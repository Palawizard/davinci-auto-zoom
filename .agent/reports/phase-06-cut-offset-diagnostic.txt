Findings
--------
1. ROOT CAUSE, measured. The forward-only search found a candidate in exactly ONE burst
   (b0, cut 216174 at +35f) and even that one is outside the 21-frame window, so it never
   snapped. Every relevant cut in this material sits a few frames BEFORE the detected
   speech end, never after. A forward-only window is structurally blind to it.

2. The offsets cluster tightly: -4, -4, -4, -5, -6, -7, -7 frames (7 bursts, 8 counting
   the two -7s separately: b0 -7, b3 -4, b6 -4, b8 -4, b9 -6, b11 -4, b12 -5, b13 -7).
   Eight of the fourteen bursts have a hard cut within 7 frames before base_reset.

3. Those eight are exactly the cases the human edit aligned on. In every burst where a cut
   sits within 7 frames before burst.end, the manual DAZ_OUTPUT_MVP x0 starts EXACTLY on
   that cut. Where no such cut exists (b1, b5, b7, b10), the manual x0 is near burst.end
   and NOT on a cut (deltas -10, -3, -6, -9 from burst.end). The rule "snap to a very close
   cut, otherwise reset directly" reproduces the human behaviour without being tuned to it.

4. The gap between signal and noise is enormous. Relevant backward offsets: 4-7 frames.
   The next-nearest backward cut in any other burst is 30 frames (b4), then 114 (b10),
   139 (b5), 148 (b7), 151 (b1), 228 (b2). A lookback anywhere in [7f, 29f] gives the
   identical result on this material.

Chosen value
------------
cut_snap_lookback_ms = 120  ->  7 frames at 60 fps.

  - 100 ms (the suggested conservative baseline) is 6 frames and would MISS b0 and b13,
    both at -7. It is measurably too small for this material.
  - 120 ms is the smallest round value that covers every observed case (max |delta| = 7).
  - The margin to the first false positive is ~4x (next candidate at 30 frames = 500 ms)
    and ~16x to the first genuinely unrelated cut (114 frames = 1900 ms).
  - It stays far below the 350 ms forward window, so the rule keeps its conservative bias
    towards "after the speech".

This value was chosen from the cut/burst-end offsets alone. It was NOT tuned to maximise
any similarity score against DAZ_OUTPUT_MVP; the manual column is reported only to show
that the measured rule agrees with a human editor, which is corroboration, not the target.

Expected effect on the plan (predicted before implementing)
-----------------------------------------------------------
14 reset_x0 total: 8 become reset_cut_snap_backward (b0, b3, b6, b8, b9, b11, b12, b13),
6 remain reset_direct (b1, b2, b4, b5, b7, b10), 0 forward snaps. Every one of the 8
candidate cuts leaves far more than the 15 frames the x0 animation needs before the next
burst's zoom, and every shortened x1 stays well above its own 15-frame animation, so no
suppression is expected.
