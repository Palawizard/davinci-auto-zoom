2b. THE GUARD THAT FIRED, AND WHY THAT IS THE HEADLINE
-------------------------------------------------------

The first attempt at the secondary-audio render **refused**:

    VoiceRenderFailed: the surviving audio track(s) are [('Audio 1', 21), ('Audio 2', 21)],
    expected [('Audio 2', 21), ('Audio 3', 21)]; the wrong track would have been analysed

Deleting A1 to leave A2+A3 renumbers *and renames* the survivors on 21.0.4.5. Since all three
audio tracks here carry the same 21 items from the same source, the post-condition could no
longer prove which track had survived — and it stopped rather than rendering a mix it could
not identify. Nothing was left behind by the refusal; cleanup ran as normal.

`_silence_audio_tracks` (empty the unwanted tracks with `DeleteClips`, keep every index, name
and item count intact) replaced the delete for this case. `_isolate_voice_track` is untouched
and still runs the Phase 3 single-track path. See D063.

`gameplay-study` takes a few minutes (three renders, VAD, envelope, ffmpeg video decode). Run
it without a short command timeout, for the same reason as `apply-preview`: the safety model
is transactional against *failures*, not against the process being killed. A kill mid-run
leaves a `DAZ_AUDIO_SCRATCH_*` timeline and possibly a temporary render preset, both disposable
by name — because the thing that would clean them up is the thing that got killed.
