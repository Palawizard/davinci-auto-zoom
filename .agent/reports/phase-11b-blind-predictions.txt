4.3 THE ONE RULE THE DATA ACTUALLY SUPPORTS, AND THE ONE IT REFUSES

SUPPORTED — **every hard cut that happens while the picture is at FACE_X3 carries a reset**:

    216416   216580   217257   217654   218398   218631        6 / 6

There is no counterexample in the development set: FACE_X3 never survives a hard cut. The two
off-cut resets say the same thing from the other side — 216758 leaves a FACE_X3 entered at
216682 whose next cut is 216812, and 216991 leaves a FACE_X3 entered at 216919 whose next cut
is 217055. Both are placed BACKWARDS from that cut so the re-entry can land on it. Counting
those, the rule is 8 / 8.

NO THRESHOLD IS NEEDED, and this is a measurement rather than a simplification. The FACE_X3
holds at those cuts are 59, 49, 35, 24, 29 and 46 frames; the rule "FACE_X3 held >= T" gives
the IDENTICAL development result for every T from 0 to 24 frames, because no cut at FACE_X3
is ever declined. T = 0 is chosen: it is the whole plateau, and it adds no parameter.
The user's hypothesis "X3 for exactly N seconds -> reset" is therefore not what the edit
contains. What it contains is "X3 does not survive a cut".

REFUSED — the same-thought resets at FACE_X2 (217373, 218235, 218735) are NOT separable by
any counter measured here. Their values are fully nested inside the non-reset FACE_X2 cuts:

    feature          rhythm resets at FACE_X2   non-reset cuts at FACE_X2
    held             36, 41, 52                 52, 87, 53, 49
    cycle            89, 69, 68                 75, 110, 80, 93
    cuts in cycle    1, 2, 2                    2, 3, 1, 3

The distributions overlap completely. **No rhythm rule is claimed for FACE_X2.** Three of the
five development rhythm resets are therefore left unexplained on purpose, rather than fitted.

4.4 RE-ENTRY, RE-MEASURED

Phase 11a refuted the "~1 second at X0" intuition and proposed instead that the entry is
anchored and the dwell falls out. Phase 11b tested that directly on the 15 development resets
that have a next FACE_X1, by asking which label-free anchor reproduces the manual entry:

    candidate anchor                                 median |delta|   p90   max   exact
    first qualifying voice recovery >= animation end       33 fr       54   174     0/13
    first hard cut >= animation end                        55 fr      126   162     4/15
    reset + 36 frames (the development median gap)         14 fr       28    30     1/15

    manual anchor gaps: 19 22 22 23 27 31 33 36 38 45 51 62 64 65 66   median 36 (600 ms)

The result is a genuine refinement of Phase 11a: on this material NO available label-free
anchor — not a cut, not a voice recovery — reproduces the creator's re-entry better than a
constant does. So the frozen re-entry rule is the simplest one the evidence supports:

    FROZEN RE-ENTRY:  next FACE_X1 = reset frame + 36 frames, and none at all if that lands
                      at or past the island end (which is what a loop reset is).

Sensitivity, measured on the development set and reported so the choice is not mistaken for a
fit: fixed 40, fixed 45, "36 snapped to a cut within +-12 or +-20", and "first cut in
[reset+15, reset+60] else 36" all land within 0.36-0.46 exact-state agreement and 9-14 frames
of median entry error. Nothing separates them. 36 is kept because it is the measured median
and it has one parameter.

4.5 HOW GOOD IS THE SIMULATED STATE? — THE BIGGEST DECLARED WEAKNESS

The blind Short has no zoom track, so the state at each cut must be SIMULATED. Running the
simulator on the development islands with the creator's own reset decisions supplied (so that
only the promotion engine and the re-entry rule are under test):

    exact state agreement at the 28 cuts      11 / 28   =  0.393
    face-versus-X0 agreement (the gate)       23 / 28   =  0.821

Read honestly: **the simulated ladder position is wrong more often than it is right**, and the
five gate errors are all "the simulator had already re-entered FACE_X1 while the creator was
still at X0". This is declared here, before unblinding, because it directly limits P2 and P3:
the rhythm rule fires on `state == FACE_X3`, and that state is only as good as the simulation.

Phase 11a's family H found that gating on a HELD face state buys +0.21 precision. That result
used the creator's own track. With a simulated state it does not reproduce — see 4.6.

5. THE FROZEN RUBRIC
====================

Reproduced from `tools/research/phase11b/annotations.py`, which is the committed artefact.

Input: at most ten aligned words each side of the cut, scoped to the cut's own island.
Output: one class, one subtype, one confidence (high / medium / low).

    DISCOURSE_BOUNDARY        the words after the cut OPEN a new discourse unit
      ANSWER_TO_HOOK          the Short's opening question/premise ends, the body begins
      NEW_TOPIC               a new subject is introduced
      NEW_STEP                a new step of a described procedure is announced
      CONSEQUENCE             a new sentence stating the result of what precedes
      CONCLUSION              a wrap-up, a summary, or a call to action
      CONTRAST                an explicit adversative opening a new sentence

    SAME_THOUGHT_CONTINUES    the current discourse unit is still running
      EXPLANATION_CONTINUES   the same proposition continues
      SUBORDINATE_CLAUSE      the words after the cut open a subordinate clause of it
      APPOSITION              they name or qualify the thing just introduced
      LIST_CONTINUES          another item of a list already running
      EXAMPLE_CONTINUES       an illustration of the current point
      TAKE_SPLICE             a hesitation or false start removed; the clause resumes

    AMBIGUOUS                 neither reading is clearly better. NEVER predicts a reset.

    R1  a boundary needs a new SENTENCE-LEVEL unit after the cut. A conjunction sitting
        mid-clause (`et`, `mais`, `du coup`) does not create one. Phase 11a family D is the
        evidence: those words appear at non-boundaries just as often.
    R2  boundary requires BOTH: the words before end a complete proposition, and the words
        after start a new one.
    R3  a word split across the cut is irrelevant to the class (11a: median 3-frame overhang,
        a trim habit rather than a discourse fact).
    R4  the last cut of an island is annotated on its text like any other. The loop reset is a
        policy override, not a semantic class.
    R5  when a sentence boundary falls strictly BETWEEN two consecutive hard cuts, it is
        assigned to the nearer cut by word time and the other cut is a continuation; ties go
        to the earlier cut. Shorts 1 and 2 put their boundaries on cuts directly, so this rule
        was never exercised there. It is written down because Short 3's geometry needs it and
        an unwritten tie-break is not reproducible.

This rubric is the `AGENT_SEMANTIC_REASONING` signal. It is NOT computable by DAZ today, and
nothing in this phase pretends otherwise.


6. THE FROZEN CANDIDATES
========================

All four are gated by the transition graph: a cut where the SIMULATED state is X0 can never
receive a reset, because `x0 -> x0` is not a move the graph has. That gate is structure, not a
learned rule.

    P0   semantic only
         hard cut AND rubric class == DISCOURSE_BOUNDARY  ->  SEMANTIC_RESET

    P1   P0 + deterministic loop
         last hard cut of the island                      ->  LOOP_RESET   (outranks P0)

    P2   P0 + rhythm refresh
         simulated state == FACE_X3 at a hard cut         ->  RHYTHM_REFRESH_RESET

    P3   P0 + rhythm + loop, in precedence loop > semantic > rhythm

    BASELINE   every hard cut the graph allows a reset at

STATEFUL BY CONSTRUCTION. Each candidate is walked chronologically through its own island and
every counter it reads — state, hold, cycle length, cuts since the entry — is derived from its
OWN earlier predictions. No candidate ever sees a later frame or a manual placement.

FROZEN SIMULATOR SETTINGS, none of them retuned (task §37):

    promotion_min_drop_db 20   promotion_recovery_within_db 6
    promotion_min_valley_ms 30 promotion_max_valley_ms 650
    transition_frames 15       zoom cut snap +-7 frames (120 ms)
    energy window/hop/smoothing 30/10/30 ms      Silero defaults 0.5/250/100/30

One deliberate deviation from the shipped planner, stated because it is real: the shipped
`_zoom_chain` refuses a promotion that cannot be held for `promotion_min_hold_ms` before the
reset **that it already knows about**. A causal simulator does not know where the next reset
will be — that is the object of the study — so that one check is dropped. Every other
promotion condition is kept.
