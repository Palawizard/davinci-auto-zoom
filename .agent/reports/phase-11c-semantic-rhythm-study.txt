3. SEMANTIC RUBRIC V2 — the concession category the V1 rubric did not have
=========================================================================

3.1 WHY 219525 WAS A REAL MISS

The V1 rubric classified it `SAME_THOUGHT_CONTINUES / SUBORDINATE_CLAUSE`. Grammatically that
is correct: what follows the cut IS a subordinate clause of the sentence before it. Editorially
it is wrong, and the creator says why — the clause is a concessive pivot, the "even if..."
shape. V1 had no category between "a new sentence starts" and "the same clause runs on", so a
pivot inside one sentence had nowhere to go.

3.2 THE V2 REVISION

V2 keeps every V1 category and adds one, plus a second reported separately:

    CONTRAST_OR_CONCESSION    an adversative or concessive pivot that reorients the discourse,
                              whether or not it opens a new sentence          reset candidate
    REFORMULATION             a corrective restatement of what was just said  reset candidate,
                              ONE occurrence, reported apart for that reason

and it separates two things V1 collapsed:

    the SEMANTIC CATEGORY        what the discourse does at this cut
    the RESET CANDIDATE flag     whether that is worth returning to X0 for

`CONTRAST_OR_CONCESSION` is a category, not a trigger. It is a reset candidate only when the
pivot OPENS the clause after the cut and functions as a pivot there.

3.3 THE EVIDENCE, POSITIVE AND NEGATIVE

Every concessive / adversative / reformulation marker in the three Shorts — all of them, not
the convenient ones:

    217055     0     217055          0                 yes         216991 (entry-first)
    217381     0     217373          8                 yes         217373
    217838     0     217835          3                 yes         217835 (loop cut)

**Position is the whole discriminator, exactly as Phase 11a's family D warned.** The one
negative case carries the same word as two of the positives and is an intensifier inside a
running clause. The six positives sit 0-8 frames after their cut and the negative sits at 19;
nothing lies between. That 10-frame separation is a DESCRIPTION of seven points, not a tuned
threshold, and seven points is a small sample. Two of the six are loop cuts that the
deterministic loop rule already owns, so the semantic layer gains four.

NO WORDLIST RULE IS PROPOSED, and none should be built. What transfers is the CATEGORY: does
the clause after the cut turn the statement around? A rubric asks that; a `startswith()` does
not.

4. RHYTHM — the resets are PROSPECTIVE, and that is measurable
==============================================================

4.1 THE HYPOTHESIS THE CREATOR HANDED US

219784 is not "I have been at X3 too long". It is "go back to X0 so I have the room to do
X1 -> X2 -> X3 again over the long phrase coming up". So the quantity is not how long a state
has been held, it is how much visual progression is still available.

    ZoomHeadroomFeatures (tools/research/phase11c/headroom.py)

        current_state, current_rung
        time_since_last_reset, time_since_last_x1, time_in_current_state
        horizon                    the next strong discourse boundary, or the island end.
                                   NEVER a fixed number of seconds
        future_discourse_duration, future_speech_duration
        future_qualifying_recoveries
        promotions_available_without_reset      the KEEP branch
        promotions_available_after_reset        the RESET branch
        headroom_gain = after - without

    KEEP    hold the current state, consume the future recovery cues, climb while a rung is
            left
    RESET   go to X0 now, re-enter FACE_X1 at reset+36, consume the cues from the new cycle

Both branches are pure arithmetic over the island geometry and the energy envelope's qualifying
voice recoveries. The only labelled input is the CURRENT STATE, which is why §6 exists.

4.3 WHY THE THRESHOLD IS 1 AND NOT A FITTED NUMBER

`gain >= 0` fires on all ten controls (every control has gain exactly 0): precision collapses
to 4/14. `gain >= 2` keeps precision at 1.000 and drops recall to 2/7. The step between 0 and 1
is where every control sits, so N=1 is a boundary in the data, not a knob. In words: **reset
when resetting would unlock at least one promotion that keeping would not.**

LEAVE-ONE-SHORT-OUT, re-deriving N from two Shorts and applying it to the third:

    train Shorts 2+3  ->  N=1    held-out Short 1:  TP 1/2   FP 0/4
    train Shorts 1+3  ->  N=1    held-out Short 2:  TP 1/3   FP 0/2
    train Shorts 1+2  ->  N=1    held-out Short 3:  TP 2/2   FP 0/4

N is the same integer in all three folds and no fold produces a false positive. This is not
proof of generalisation — the annotation behind the horizon is not blind, and three folds of
one creator is nothing — but it does rule out an obviously overfitted threshold.

4.4 WHERE THE GATE IS OPEN AT ALL, frame by frame

Because 219784 and 220442 are OFF-cut, the gate has to be evaluated at every frame, not only at
cuts. Over the three islands it is open in 18 separated windows.

    **18 of 18 open windows are followed by a manual reset**, at 0-109 frames from the window's
    end. There is no window in three Shorts where the gate opens and the creator does nothing.

One honest qualification, and it matters: the window END is an artefact. The state used here is
the creator's own, so the gate necessarily closes when he resets. Only the window START and the
window COUNT carry information. The reading that survives it is: the gate is not noisy — it
does not chatter in places the creator leaves alone — but it is far from sufficient on its own,
because 18 windows cannot choose between 28 resets and it is silent at most of them.

6. STATE RECONSTRUCTION — and the root cause is not the reset history
=====================================================================

Phase 11b measured that its causal simulator agrees with the creator's ladder position 11/28 of
the time and declared it the weak link. It did not know WHY. Task §13 asked for a root cause
before any fix, and the answer changes what a fix would even mean.

6.1 ORACLE CYCLES — hand the engine the creator's own cycle boundaries

Every manual cycle (his FACE_X1 entry -> his next reset) was replayed with the promotion engine
alone: the same qualifying recoveries, the same 15-frame animation gate, the same 7-frame zoom
cut snap. The reset policy, the re-entry policy and the state history are all removed from the
problem. The ONLY thing left to get wrong is which recovery becomes which promotion.

    cycles                                          28
    manual promotions                               36
    simulated promotions                            32
    cycles with the same promotion COUNT            19
    cycles the engine over-promotes                  4
    cycles the engine under-promotes                 5
    cycles where the creator promoted with NO qualifying recovery at all   4
    frame error on the 24 count-matched promotions  median 15  p90 38  max 81

**Nine of 28 cycles get the wrong number of promotions even with perfect cycle boundaries, and
the ones that get the count right land a median of 15 frames (250 ms) away.** In four cycles the
creator promoted where the energy envelope offers no qualifying valley at all.

6.2 LABEL-FREE — the full Phase 11b simulator, re-measured on all three Shorts

    exact state agreement at hard cuts       13 / 42
    coarse agreement (face versus X0)        35 / 42

6.3 THE CONCLUSION

The reset history is not the bottleneck. **The promotion cue stream is.** A perfect reset policy
fed into this engine would still put the ladder in the wrong place, because the mapping from
"qualifying voice recovery" to "the creator's zoom promotion" does not hold on this material.

Two things follow, and both are constraints rather than results:

  - **no production threshold was touched.** `promotion_min_drop_db = 20`, the 6 dB recovery
    window, 30/650 ms valley bounds and `promotion_min_hold_ms = 400` are exactly as Phase 10
    left them. This phase measured the existing logic in a multi-cycle setting; it did not
    retune it, and it must not be retuned to fit `bluescreen 2` (task §14);
  - **anything that needs the ladder position is ORACLE_ONLY today.** That includes the whole
    rhythm layer of §4, and it is the single reason this phase does not freeze it for a blind
    retest.

An honest caveat on top: Phases 11a and 11b both took the creator's word that his X1/X2/X3
behaviour matches the shipped planner. §6.1 is the first measurement of that claim, and it does
not support it. Whether the gap is the detector, the creator, or the research simulator's
dropped `promotion_min_hold` check is NOT established here.

The word-start column looks convincing and it is worth nothing. CHANCE BASELINE, computed over
every frame of the three islands: the distance to the nearest word start has **median 4.0,
mean 4.60, p90 10**. The measured resets sit at 2-6. **A word boundary is not an anchor here;
the transcript is simply dense enough that everything is near one.** Reporting it the other way
round would have been the easiest mistake in this phase to make.

No anchor was found for the two off-cut rhythm resets. `WHEN to refresh` (§4) and `WHERE to put
the reset` are therefore separate problems, and only the first has an answer.

7.2 ENTRY-FIRST PLACEMENT — measured, and now 4/6

Four of the six off-cut resets are placed EARLY so that the next FACE_X1 lands on a cut:

    reset 216758 -> entry 216823, cut 216812   entry is 11 frames after the cut
    reset 216991 -> entry 217055, cut 217055   entry is EXACTLY on the cut
    reset 219097 -> entry 219193, cut 219179   entry is 14 frames after the cut
    reset 220243 -> entry 220316, cut 220316   entry is EXACTLY on the cut

The other two (219784, 220442) put their entry 79 and 95 frames from the nearest cut. The gap
between 14 and 79 is total: there is nothing in between, which is why the anchoring window in
§2 is 15 frames and why it is a description rather than a threshold.

So an entry-first model — choose the FACE_X1 anchor, then derive the reset backwards — is what
the creator does for the semantic family, and it is exactly the two "false positives" the blind
P0 was charged for. A cut-anchored placement model is wrong for 4 of 26 anchored resets.

13. VERDICT
===========

What this phase established:

  - the creator's four answers turn "36% unexplained" into "one visual reset, one semantic miss
    with a nameable cause, two prospective rhythm refreshes";
  - rubric V2's `CONTRAST_OR_CONCESSION` is a real, transferable category with 6/6 clause-initial
    positives and one mid-clause negative, and it repairs two known misses;
  - rhythm IS prospective and it IS measurable: `headroom_gain >= 1` separates 4 of 7 rhythm
    resets from 10 of 10 controls with zero false positives, the threshold survives
    leave-one-Short-out, and all 18 gate windows are followed by a reset;
  - the loop rule stays exact;
  - and the rhythm layer cannot be computed without the ladder position, which §6 shows is
    broken at the promotion engine and not at the reset policy.

So the rhythm CONCEPT is in better shape than at any point in this research, and the machinery
that would let a tool use it is in worse shape than we thought, because the fault is now located
in a component this phase is forbidden to change and that no reset policy can compensate for.
