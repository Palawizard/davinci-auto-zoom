4. READING THOSE NUMBERS
------------------------

**Peak level: 11 of 14 cycles agree with the human.** All three divergences were predicted by
the MVP2 analysis before the planner was run, and none of them is a promotion-rule failure:

  cycle 1  — auto x3, manual x1. The automatic reset lands 77 frames late (the burst-extent
             problem carried from Phase 6), which makes the automatic cycle 149 frames against
             the human's 74. A 149-frame cycle promoting twice is the rule working correctly
             on the wrong burst.
  cycle 12 — auto x3, manual x1. Same cause from the other end: this is the one burst built
             from two speech segments, and the planner opens it 123 frames before the human
             does. 163 automatic frames against 40 manual.
  cycle 7  — auto x3, manual x2. The genuine human nuance. This is the LONGEST cycle in the
             timeline (204 manual frames) and the editor deliberately held it at x2, while
             taking the 138-frame closing cycle to x3. No monotonic duration rule can do both.
             Documented in the analysis report, not fixed, not worked around.

Fixing burst extent — a Phase 9 candidate — would resolve 2 of the 3 by itself.

**Promotion timing, where both sides promoted, is close.** Median x1->x2 delta is +1 frame
across 6 cycles, and 4 of the 6 are within 21 frames. The two outliers are cycles 7 (-69) and
0 (+18), and cycle 7's is again inherited from its 41-frame-early start.

The single x2->x3 comparison available lands within **1 frame** of the human (auto 219097,
manual 219098). One data point, calibrated on that same data point — reported for completeness,
not as validation.

**Reset delta median is 0 frames**, with 8 of 14 exactly equal. The two non-trivial values
(+77, -10) are the known burst-extent cases.

**Level counts overall:** auto 6/4/4 against manual 8/5/1. The tool promotes more readily than
this editor does, concentrated entirely in x3 (4 vs 1). Three of the four automatic x3s are the
three divergent cycles above.
