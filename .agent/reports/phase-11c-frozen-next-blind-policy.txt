PHASE 11c — FROZEN POLICY FOR THE NEXT BLIND TEST
2026-08-20 · branch dev · RESEARCH, NOT SHIPPED

THIS FILE IS IMMUTABLE FROM THE COMMIT THAT INTRODUCES IT. It is the complete statement of what
will be predicted on the next genuinely unseen Short, written down before that Short exists as
data. A defect found in it later is REPORTED, never repaired in place — the same rule that
protects `phase-11b-blind-predictions.txt`.

It must NOT be run on `bluescreen 2`'s fourth island. That island is extra clips kept for
later, it is not a completed Short, and it is out of scope for every phase so far. Using it
would spend the only unused material in the project on a test it cannot answer.

Everything below was derived on Shorts 1-3 of `bluescreen 2`, which are ALL development data.


0. WHAT IS AND IS NOT IN THIS POLICY
====================================

    LAYER 1  LOOP        IN, deterministic
    LAYER 2  SEMANTIC    IN, agent reasoning over a word-aligned transcript
    PLACEMENT + RE-ENTRY IN, deterministic
    STATE GATE           IN, coarse, computed from the policy's own history only

    LAYER 3  RHYTHM      **STATED IN FULL IN §5 AND EXCLUDED FROM THE PREDICTION.** It needs
                         the ladder position, and `phase-11c-semantic-rhythm-study.txt` §6
                         shows that position cannot be reconstructed: the promotion engine gets
                         the promotion COUNT wrong in 9 of 28 cycles even when the cycle
                         boundaries are handed to it. A rule that cannot be executed cannot be
                         blind-tested, and pretending otherwise would score the simulator
                         instead of the rule.

    LAYER 4  VISUAL      OUT. Not observable from audio or text. Its resets are expected
                         misses and are excluded only from the ADDRESSABLE metric, and only
                         when the creator confirms them AFTER the predictions are committed.


1. INPUTS THE POLICY IS ALLOWED
===============================

    the reference video track V1 of the blind timeline (clips, hence hard cuts and islands)
    the rendered voice audio of that timeline
    a WhisperX word-aligned French transcript of it (large-v3, language=fr, forced alignment)
    the policy's OWN earlier decisions

    FORBIDDEN: the manual zoom track, in any form, until the predictions are committed.


2. STRUCTURE
============

    content island       maximal run of V1 clips with no gap >= 30 frames
    hard cut             `previous.end == next.start` exactly. Nothing else is a cut.
    scope                every island of the blind Short is analysed alone. No word, cue,
                         state or counter crosses an island boundary.


3. LAYER 1 — LOOP (deterministic override)
==========================================

    The LAST hard cut of a content island receives a reset to X0, and no FACE_X1 re-entry
    follows it inside that island.

    Evidence: 3/3, delta 0, on three Shorts. Stated by the creator in advance.
    Precedence: highest. If the last cut is also a discourse boundary, the reason reported is
    LOOP and the frame is the same either way.


4. LAYER 2 — SEMANTIC, RUBRIC V2
================================

Input per cut: at most ten aligned words each side, scoped to the cut's own island.
Output per cut: one CATEGORY, one RESET-CANDIDATE flag, one CONFIDENCE.

4.1 CATEGORIES, and whether each is a reset candidate

    DISCOURSE_BOUNDARY family                                       reset candidate
      ANSWER_TO_HOOK            the opening question/premise ends, the body begins    yes
      NEW_TOPIC                 a new subject is introduced                           yes
      NEW_STEP                  a new step of a described procedure is announced      yes
      CONSEQUENCE               a new sentence stating the result of what precedes    yes
      CONCLUSION                a wrap-up, a summary, or a call to action             yes
      CONTRAST                  an explicit adversative opening a new sentence        yes
      CONTRAST_OR_CONCESSION    NEW IN V2. An adversative or concessive PIVOT that    yes
                                reorients the discourse, whether or not it opens a
                                new sentence
      REFORMULATION             NEW IN V2. A corrective restatement of what was       yes
                                just said

    SAME_THOUGHT_CONTINUES family                                   reset candidate
      EXPLANATION_CONTINUES / EXAMPLE_CONTINUES / LIST_CONTINUES     no
      SUBORDINATE_CLAUSE / APPOSITION / TAKE_SPLICE                  no

    AMBIGUOUS                   neither reading is clearly better                     no

4.2 DECISION RULES, unchanged from V1 unless marked

    R1  a boundary needs a new SENTENCE-LEVEL unit after the cut. A conjunction sitting
        mid-clause does not create one — position and function, not word presence.
    R2  boundary requires BOTH: the words before end a complete proposition, and the words
        after start a new one.
    R3  a word split across the cut is irrelevant to the class.
    R4  the last cut of an island is annotated on its text like any other. The loop reset is a
        policy override, not a semantic class.
    R5  a sentence boundary falling strictly BETWEEN two consecutive hard cuts is assigned to
        the nearer cut by word time; ties go to the earlier cut.
    R6  NEW IN V2. `CONTRAST_OR_CONCESSION` and `REFORMULATION` are exempt from R1 and R2: a
        pivot inside one sentence can still be a boundary. They apply ONLY when the pivot
        OPENS the clause after the cut, which in three Shorts meant it began within
        **10 frames** of the cut; a marker further inside a running clause is not a pivot.
    R7  NEW IN V2. The category is decided by FUNCTION in context, never by matching a word.
        No word list is part of this policy, and none may be added to it.

4.3 CONFIDENCE

    `high` / `medium` / `low`, the annotator's own. Confidence does NOT change the prediction:
    a `low` boundary still predicts a reset. It is recorded so the failure analysis can ask
    afterwards whether the errors were the ones already flagged.


5. LAYER 3 — RHYTHM. STATED, AND NOT PART OF THE PREDICTION
===========================================================

Written down in full so that it is falsifiable the day the ladder position can be computed.

    at a candidate frame, with the CURRENT LADDER STATE known:

      horizon      = the next reset-candidate cut of layer 2, else the island end
      KEEP         = hold the current state; consume the qualifying voice recoveries before
                     the horizon, respecting the 15-frame animation gate; climb while a rung
                     remains
      RESET        = X0 now, FACE_X1 at frame+36, then the same consumption from FACE_X1
      headroom_gain = promotions(RESET) - promotions(KEEP)

      FIRE when headroom_gain >= 1.

    THRESHOLD: 1, and it is the only number. Leave-one-Short-out re-derives 1 in all three
    folds with zero false positives.
    PRECEDENCE if it were ever enabled: LOOP -> SEMANTIC -> RHYTHM.
    KNOWN LIMIT: it explains 4 of 7 rhythm resets. The three misses are FACE_X2 resets with
    gain 0 or negative, and nothing in this research explains those.
    PLACEMENT: unsolved. The gate opens 28-107 frames before the creator's reset, and no anchor
    was found inside the window (a word boundary is 4 frames from ANY frame on this material).

    IT WILL NOT FIRE IN THE NEXT BLIND TEST.


6. STATE GATE — coarse, and from the policy's own history only
==============================================================

    The policy tracks ONE bit: is the picture at X0, or in a face state.

      the island opens at X0 and enters FACE_X1 at the island's first frame;
      a reset sets X0 at its frame;
      the re-entry of §8 sets "face" at its frame.

    A reset is refused when the bit says X0. Nothing else gates it. The promotion engine is
    NOT consulted, and the rung (X1/X2/X3) is never used by this policy.

    Event order at one frame: CUT, then ENTRY. A cut receiving the re-entry is therefore
    evaluated while the bit is still X0, and no reset is placed there.

    Measured coarse agreement with the creator on three Shorts: 35/42.


7. RESET PLACEMENT
==================

    ENTRY-FIRST. For a reset-candidate cut C:

      the FACE_X1 re-entry is anchored to C;
      the reset is placed at C - 36 frames (the re-entry rule of §8, run backwards);
      if C - 36 falls at or before the island start, or before the previous re-entry, the
        reset is placed on C itself instead.

    Evidence: 4 of the 6 off-cut resets in three Shorts are placed early so their entry lands
    on a cut (entry 0, 0, 11 and 14 frames from it); no other off-cut reset's entry is nearer
    than 79 frames to a cut.

    TIE-BREAKS
      two reset-candidate cuts closer together than 36 frames: keep the EARLIER one and drop
        the later, because the later falls inside the X0 window of the earlier;
      a loop cut always wins over a semantic cut at the same frame;
      a reset is never placed before the current cycle's FACE_X1 entry.


8. FACE_X1 RE-ENTRY
===================

    entry = reset + 36 frames, and no entry is made if it falls at or past the island end
    (that is the loop reset; the Short ends at X0).

    Evidence over 25 entries and three Shorts: median error 9 frames, p90 29, max 60. No
    label-free alternative beats it — the first hard cut after the animation misses by a median
    of 42 frames, the first qualifying recovery by 27, and the first word start after reset+30
    ties it at 8 while being 4 frames from any frame at all. The gap does not split by reset
    reason (semantic median 36, rhythm 39), so ONE rule is used for every reset.

    36 IS A BASELINE, NOT A LAW. Its p90 error is half a second and that is visible.


9. HOW THE NEXT BLIND TEST MUST BE SCORED
=========================================

    universe        the hard cuts of the blind Short
    positive        a cut with a manual reset ANCHORED to it — on it, or with the manual
                    FACE_X1 landing within 15 frames of it
    reported apart  DECISION (precision/recall over that universe) and FRAME (|delta| for each
                    correct decision). They are never merged into one number.
    strict          every manual reset counts
    addressable     creator-confirmed VISUAL resets excluded, and ONLY those. A reset the
                    policy missed is never reclassified as visual to excuse the miss.
    also reported   resets with no anchoring cut at all — unreachable by this policy by
                    construction, and counted as misses in the strict reading.

    The predictions, the annotation of every cut and the frames must be COMMITTED before the
    blind Short's zoom track is read at all.
