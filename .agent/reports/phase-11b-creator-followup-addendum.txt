3. NEW INTERPRETATION
=====================

    ONE reset (219354) is visual. The transcript is not supposed to predict it, and it is the
    only reset in three Shorts that this research direction can never address.

    ONE reset (219525) is a genuine SEMANTIC MISS. The frozen V1 rubric called it
    SAME_THOUGHT_CONTINUES / SUBORDINATE_CLAUSE, which is grammatically right and editorially
    wrong: a concessive clause can turn the thought around without opening a new sentence. The
    rubric had no category for that. Phase 11c adds one (`CONTRAST_OR_CONCESSION`).

    TWO resets (219784, 220442) are rhythm refreshes, and the creator's reason for 219784 is
    PROSPECTIVE — he resets to create future room to climb, not because a state has been held
    too long. Phase 11c measures that directly.

    So the honest figure is no longer "36% unexplained". Of Short 3's eleven resets: nine are
    text- or structure-addressable in principle, one is visual, and none is a mystery.


4. WHAT THIS CHANGES
====================

  - the direction of the research. "Is there a ceiling?" becomes "can a prospective rhythm
    rule and a concession category be written down?" — which is Phase 11c;
  - the reset taxonomy of Short 3, in a NEW layer
    (`tools/research/phase11c/creator_feedback.py`), never by editing Phase 11b's;
  - the evaluation target: a creator-confirmed visual reset may now be excluded from an
    ADDRESSABLE coverage metric. Exactly one reset qualifies, and no reset may ever be excluded
    because a model missed it.

    the taxonomy, and the edit has not moved;

  - that Short 3 was used for no tuning before it was unblinded;
  - the verdict line of the blind report, `SEMANTICS HELP BUT GENERALISATION WEAK`. That was
    the honest verdict at that moment. Phase 11c's verdict is a separate, later statement.
