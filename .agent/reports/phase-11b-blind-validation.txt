6. THE RHYTHM RESULT — DOES "DO NOT STAY AT X3" HELP?
=====================================================

Short answer: **as shipped in P2/P3 it made things worse**, and the reason is not the rule.

6.1 THE RULE ITSELF STILL HOLDS

In Short 3, FACE_X3 is entered four times and NEVER survives a hard cut. Every X3 period ends
in a reset (219999 and 220608 on the cut, 220243 and 220442 off it). Combined with the
development set the rule is now:

    hard cuts held at FACE_X3 that carry a reset      6 / 6  (dev)   2 / 2  (Short 3)
    FACE_X3 periods ending in a reset                 8 / 8  (dev)   4 / 4  (Short 3)

Zero counterexamples in three Shorts. And still no threshold is needed: the X3 holds at those
cuts are 24-59 frames in the development set and 29-102 in Short 3, with no non-reset case at
any duration to separate from.

6.2 BUT IT ADDS NOTHING, AND ITS IMPLEMENTATION SUBTRACTED

With the CREATOR's own state, the rule fires at exactly two Short 3 cuts — 219999 and 220608 —
and both are ALREADY DISCOURSE_BOUNDARY cuts that P0 predicts. Contribution: +0 true positives,
+0 false positives. The rhythm rule is *correct and redundant* on this Short.

What P2/P3 actually did was fire at 219417, 219525 and 220579, where the SIMULATED state said
FACE_X3 and the creator was at FACE_X1, FACE_X2 and FACE_X2. One of those (219525) happened to
be a real reset; two were not, and a third consequence — the rhythm reset at 220579 — put P2/P3
at X0 so they lost the genuine semantic reset at 220608. Net: -0.16 precision, -0.09 F1.

**Every point P2/P3 lost is traceable to the simulated ladder position, not to the rhythm
hypothesis.** That weakness was measured and declared in the checkpoint (§4.5 there: exact
state agreement 11/28 on the development set), which is what makes this attribution safe to
make rather than a convenient excuse.

8. VISUAL-PRESENTATION RESETS — WHAT COULD AND COULD NOT BE ESTABLISHED
======================================================================

The creator warned that some Short 3 resets exist for a purely visual reason: to show the
avatar in full. Four resets here (219354, 219525, 219784, 220442) are explained by no text
signal, and Short 3 is precisely the Short about the VR hardware worn on the body, so the
warning is plausible on its face.

WHAT WAS DONE. The source media was located from the Media Pool (read-only) and single frames
were extracted locally with ffmpeg at four unexplained resets and four controls, then looked
at. They were temporary, gitignored and deleted; no frame is committed (`AGENTS.md`).

WHAT WAS FOUND. The source footage of Short 3 is ONE continuous full-body avatar shot on a
blue background. Its framing does not change at a reset, at a cut, or anywhere else — the whole
Short is the same camera on the same subject. What changes is only the DAZ zoom composited on
V2, and that composite cannot be observed here: rendering it would be a mutation outside the
allowed surface, and the Fusion graph is never opened (D007).

CONCLUSION, and it is a limit rather than a result:

  - no reset can be PROVED to be a VISUAL_PRESENTATION_RESET from the evidence available;
  - therefore NO reset is excluded, and the transcript-addressable subset evaluation
    (evaluation B) is **not reported at all**. §4 is the whole result;
  - the four unexplained resets are recorded as RHYTHM_REFRESH_RESET, which is a statement
    about the TEXT (the sentence was still running), not a claim that rhythm caused them. One
    or more of them may well be visual.

What this bounds: **at least 4 of the 11 manual resets in Short 3 (36%) are not derivable from
a word-aligned transcript plus the rhythm counters measured here.** That is the ceiling on the
whole transcript direction as it currently stands, measured rather than asserted.

No computer vision, motion analysis, VLM, avatar detector or vision model was built, and
Phase 9 stays retired (task §27).

14. VERDICT
===========

What transferred, blind, on unseen material:

  - the discourse-boundary rubric. Every one of P0's seven predictions corresponds to a real
    manual reset (7/7), it found all six semantic resets (6/6), and its strict F1 of 0.714 is
    HIGHER than the 0.552 it scored on the development set where it could see the answers;
  - the loop rule, now 3/3 with zero error;
  - the re-entry constant, at a 9.5-frame median error it was not tuned for.

What did not:

  - the simulated ladder position, which agrees with the creator's only 11/28 of the time and
    which cost P2/P3 everything they lost;
  - the cut-anchored placement, which is right for 5 of 7 correct decisions and misses the two
    early-anchored resets by 82 and 73 frames;
  - the rhythm refresh as a predictor. The RULE is intact (X3 never survives a cut, 3/3 Shorts,
    zero counterexamples) but it is REDUNDANT with the semantics on Short 3 and its simulated
    form actively hurt;
  - coverage. Four of eleven manual resets (36%) are explained by nothing in this study, and it
    could not be proved whether they are visual, rhythmic or something else.

A system built on this today would place roughly two thirds of the creator's resets, put most
of them on the right frame, and miss a third of his edit entirely for reasons the transcript
cannot see. The semantics are real. The system is not there.

15. RECOMMENDED PHASE 11c — THE SMALLEST STEP THE BLIND RESULT JUSTIFIES
========================================================================

NOT implemented here, and deliberately narrow.

    Ask the creator to mark, on Short 3's four unexplained resets (219354, 219525, 219784,
    220442), whether each one exists to show the avatar. Four yes/no answers.

That is the cheapest measurement in the whole project and it decides the direction. If they are
visual, the transcript ceiling is ~64% of this edit and honest, and the next question is whether
a two-thirds-complete pass is useful to the creator at all. If they are not visual, then a
rhythm signal exists that this study failed to find, and it is worth one more measurement pass.

What should NOT be built before that answer: any profile abstraction, any reset policy in the
planner, any runtime NLP or vision dependency, any re-entry model, any state-simulation fix.
Fixing the simulator now would mean fitting it to the only blind Short that exists.
